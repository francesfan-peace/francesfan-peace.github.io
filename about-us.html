Company Overview

A manufacturer built around real market needs.

URBVO is a coating systems manufacturer based in China, serving professional flooring contractors, project buyers, building-material distributors and private-label brands in international markets. Our product range covers resinous flooring, acrylic sports surfaces, polyurethane and cementitious urethane systems, roof waterproofing, repair materials and practical coatings prepared for retail channels.

Our role is not limited to supplying a single drum of material. We help customers identify the right chemistry for the substrate and service environment, then coordinate formulation, samples, production, quality checks, packaging and export preparation. This makes it easier for project customers to work with a defined system and for distributors to build a product line that fits their own market, price point and sales channel.

Inside the factory, formulation and production teams work with batch records, sample confirmation and application checks to support repeatable output. Packaging formats can be adapted for contractor use, retail display or private-label programs, while technical documents and export files are prepared around the destination market and order requirements.

URBVO focuses on practical cooperation: clear product positioning, responsive sampling, consistent manufacturing and straightforward communication. Whether the requirement is a factory floor, a sports court, a roof waterproofing system or a distributor-owned retail range, our aim is to turn technical coating products into solutions that are easier to specify, sell and apply.
