Industrial Epoxy Flooring for Automotive Manufacturing

A smooth, seamless epoxy floor system designed to support daily production, material movement and a cleaner working environment across a large automotive facility.

Project Overview

Designed for an operating production environment

A smooth epoxy floor system was installed across 2,400 m² of production and circulation space. The seamless finish supports routine cleaning, daily pedestrian traffic and material movement, while the color zoning helps keep work and traffic areas visually organized.

System fit

  • Seamless, easy-clean finish
  • Defined production and traffic zones
  • Suitable for regular material movement
